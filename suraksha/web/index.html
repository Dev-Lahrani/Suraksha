Transparent by design.

Weather and ERA5 climatology: Open-Meteo. Air-quality model: CAMS via Open-Meteo. AQI mapping: US-EPA. Safety actions: NDMA/WHO-inspired playbooks.

  • District dots are curated HQ locations—not district boundaries.
  • Flood scores are rainfall proxies; air readings are modelled, not certified station measurements.
  • New language translations are experimental and need native-speaker review.
  • Demo data is synthetic. Missing data never means a district is safe.
  • Saved districts and checklist progress are stored on this device, not cloud-synced.
